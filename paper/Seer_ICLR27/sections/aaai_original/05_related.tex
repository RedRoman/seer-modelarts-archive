\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\noindent\textbf{Low-Light Video and Image Enhancement.} LLVE has been advanced by reflectance/illumination decomposition~\citep{stcd2025}, cross-frame attention~\citep{llve_seg}, and event-camera fusion~\citep{seeing_unseen}, spanning CNNs~\citep{stcd2025}, state-space models~\citep{bvi_mamba}, and conditional diffusion~\citep{bvi_rlv,dl_diff}; the BVI-RLV benchmark~\citep{bvi_rlv} exposed how fragile such models are across sensors and scenes. In the image regime, Retinex-transformers~\citep{retinexformer} have driven successive NTIRE winners, extended to Mamba~\citep{mamballie} and diffusion~\citep{lldiffusion} backbones. Systematic cross-dataset evaluation remains rare: models supervised via $\ell_1$/perceptual losses inherit their training set's photometric statistics, and in-domain PSNR collapses under distribution shift---the phenomenon Seer targets.

\noindent\textbf{Video Self-Supervised World Models.} V-JEPA~\citep{vjepa,vjepa2} trains a video encoder to predict masked spatio-temporal regions \emph{in embedding space} rather than pixel space, unlike pixel-reconstructing masked autoencoders such as VideoMAE~\citep{videomae}. This yields representations biased toward dynamics/layout over low-level photometric detail. V-JEPA transfers strongly to action recognition, video QA, and physical prediction; its use as a video restoration \emph{prior} is, to our knowledge, novel.

\noindent\textbf{Foundation Encoders as Restoration Priors.} Perceptual losses from frozen encoders---VGG~\citep{johnson_perceptual}, LPIPS~\citep{lpips}, DISTS~\citep{dists}, CLIP-based scores~\citep{clip_iqa}---are standard supervisory signals, and CLIP/DINO features have guided diffusion-based restoration~\citep{stablesr}, employing the encoder as a \emph{judge} that scores an already-generated candidate rather than a source of conditioning signal. Seer differs: V-JEPA is the \emph{input} prior conditioning the pixel branch via cross-attention at every scale, with a self-consistency loss closing the loop---motivated by our finding (Sec.~\ref{sec:method:invariance}) that V-JEPA is, among five modern frozen encoders, illumination-invariant to a degree competitive image encoders do not match.
