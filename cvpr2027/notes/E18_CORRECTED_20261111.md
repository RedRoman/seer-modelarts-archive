Statistics condition on these trained models. The helper checks exactly 39
unique, matched sequence names and finite metrics. It uses 10,000 paired
bootstrap draws, RNG 0, shared across metrics. Scene sensitivity resamples
source-scene clusters, retaining each terminal `_2` mate and sequence weights;
its RNG stream is separately initialized with seed 0. Intervals are not
multiple-comparison adjusted. Temporal primary scores match the repaired
`aggregate.py`: average raw prediction and GT across seeds within each
arm/sequence, then take the absolute gap. Within-seed absolute gaps determine
seed signs. The alternative mean of within-seed absolute gaps is separately
labeled a diagnostic; it is not substituted for the primary definition.
