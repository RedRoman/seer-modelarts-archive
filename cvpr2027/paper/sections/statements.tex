\section*{Reproducibility statement}
The reported result macros are generated by
\texttt{tools/make\_macros\_cvpr.py} and
\texttt{tools/make\_audit\_macros.py} from archived and separately corrected
per-sequence files. The archive includes training and evaluation code, frozen split lists,
registered decision rules and available measurements.
Section~\ref{sec:method} and App.~\ref{app:impl} describe the current model
and protocol; App.~\ref{app:membership} describes split membership.
Appendix~\ref{app:pending} identifies missing evidence and evaluations
withheld pending reconstruction checks. Historical files are preserved and
are not silently substituted for verified current results.

\section*{Ethics statement}
The work uses public research datasets (SDSD, BVI-RLV, LOL) and released model weights under their licences. Low-light enhancement can be used in surveillance; we do not target identification tasks.
