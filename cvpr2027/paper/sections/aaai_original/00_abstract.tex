Low-light video enhancement (LLVE) is inherently ill-posed and existing state-of-the-art methods, while strong within their training distribution, degrade under cross-domain shift. We observe that the recent video self-supervised world model V-JEPA is decisively more illumination-invariant than CLIP and SigLIP\,2 on paired low-light/well-lit clips (paired Wilcoxon $p{<}10^{-4}$) while being statistically tied with DINOv3 and DINOv2---suggesting a video world model is a natural illumination-stable prior for LLVE. Building on this, we introduce Seer, which anchors a Restormer-style pixel branch in a frozen V-JEPA through three complementary mechanisms: a LoRA-gated counterfactual predictor, a self-consistency loss closing the loop in feature space, and a learnable cross-layer router coupling V-JEPA's hierarchy to the pixel branch. We further derive a training-free per-channel \emph{Bayesian chroma-preservation} post-processor: under a general LMMSE formulation admitting biased, correlated and non-Gaussian residuals, the optimal fusion coefficient is a closed-form function of the joint second-moment matrix, engaged under a fixed data-independent deployment rule for cross-domain targets. Under a unified six-level out-of-distribution protocol with perceptual-first metrics (LPIPS, DISTS, temporal LPIPS) against five recent LLIE/LLVE baselines (STCD, Retinexformer, HVI-CIDNet, DarkIR, URCSA), Seer takes the PSNR crown on SDSD-indoor ($27.11$~dB) and on the zero-shot L3 LOL-v1 image split ($23.45$~dB, also winning L3 DISTS ahead of the LOL-matched HVI-CIDNet), ties Retinexformer for the SDSD-outdoor PSNR lead within the seed-variance floor ($22.03$ vs.\ $22.00$~dB), sweeps every reference-based perceptual metric on SDSD-indoor, wins LPIPS by $29\%$ against Retinexformer on SDSD-outdoor under identical training data, and produces the most temporally consistent output on both SDSD video splits ($4{\times}$ smoother than the best per-frame baseline on L0) and the strongest cross-domain temporal consistency on BVI-RLV. Zero-shot results on the LOL image splits (L3--L5)---trained purely on SDSD video---validate that the V-JEPA video prior transfers to still images without domain-specific tuning.
