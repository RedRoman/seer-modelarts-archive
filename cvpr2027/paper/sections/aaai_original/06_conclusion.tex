\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We identified V-JEPA as a top-tier illumination-invariant encoder and built \textbf{Seer}, which anchors a trainable pixel branch in frozen V-JEPA via three mechanisms plus a training-free Bayesian chroma-preservation post-processor. Under a unified six-level OOD protocol, Seer wins PSNR on L0/L3, stays within $0.34$~dB elsewhere, leads tLP on both SDSD splits and remains the strongest cross-domain method on BVI-RLV tLP---evidence that a frozen video world model is a transferable prior for low-light restoration.
