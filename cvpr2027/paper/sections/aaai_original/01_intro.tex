Low-light video enhancement (LLVE) is a fundamentally ill-posed inverse problem: state-of-the-art methods~\citep{stcd2025, bvi_mamba, bvi_rlv} learn a prior from paired data through $\ell_1$ or perceptual losses and excel \emph{within} their training distribution, but collapse under domain shift (different scene, sensor, or noise profile). We attribute this to a missing \emph{illumination-invariant representation}: if internal features drift with illumination, no amount of supervision prevents over-fitting to photometric statistics. We therefore ask whether any off-the-shelf frozen encoder already carries such invariance. Testing five candidates---CLIP~\citep{clip}, DINOv2~\citep{dinov2}, DINOv3~\citep{dinov3}, SigLIP\,2~\citep{siglip2}, and the video world model V-JEPA~\citep{vjepa,vjepa2}---we find that V-JEPA is \emph{decisively} more invariant than CLIP and SigLIP\,2 ($p{<}10^{-4}$, $d{<}{-}2.7$) and tied with DINOv3 and DINOv2 on single-frame invariance (Fig.~\ref{fig:invariance}). We break this tie with a predictive-coherence test that exercises V-JEPA's genuine pretraining objective: its predictor forecasts held-out future spatio-temporal tokens significantly better than a naive no-change baseline ($p{=}5{\times}10^{-4}$, Cohen's $d{=}1.46$ across $11$ video sequences)---a capability per-frame image encoders cannot express by construction, since they carry no temporal predictor at all; end-to-end encoder-swap ablations inside our restoration model confirm the same downstream ranking. This is not a coincidence: V-JEPA is trained to predict masked spatio-temporal regions in an \emph{embedding} space rather than in pixel space, so the objective is free to discard low-level photometric detail that is unnecessary for higher-level dynamics.

Motivated by this observation we introduce \textbf{Seer}, an LLVE framework that \emph{anchors} a trainable Restormer-style pixel branch in a frozen V-JEPA world model through three complementary mechanisms---\textbf{(M1)} a LoRA-gated \emph{counterfactual predictor} that refines V-JEPA features toward a well-lit view, \textbf{(M2)} a \emph{V-JEPA self-consistency loss} closing the loop between prior and perceptual judge, and \textbf{(M3)} a \emph{learnable cross-layer router} coupling four V-JEPA depths to the pixel branch adaptively---plus a training-free \emph{Bayesian chroma-preservation} post-processor. We formulate the fusion of Seer's prediction with a chroma-reweighted low-quality reference as a general LMMSE problem admitting biased, correlated and non-Gaussian residuals; the operational $\boldsymbol{\alpha}$ is the plug-in Bayesian estimator on empirical residual second moments, engaged under a fixed data-independent deployment rule.

To stress-test cross-domain generalization, we introduce a \emph{six-level OOD protocol} (SDSD-indoor, SDSD-outdoor, BVI-RLV, LOL-v1, LOLv2-Syn, LOLv2-Real) evaluated under a perceptual-first metric axis (LPIPS-alex, DISTS, tLP) against five recent baselines: STCD~\citep{stcd2025}, Retinexformer~\citep{retinexformer}, HVI-CIDNet~\citep{hvi_cidnet}, DarkIR~\citep{darkir}, URCSA~\citep{urcsa}. On \textbf{L0} SDSD-indoor, Seer takes the PSNR crown ($27.11$~dB) \emph{and} every reference-based perceptual metric ($35\%$ LPIPS gain, $4\times$ tLP margin over per-frame image baselines)---jointly resolving the perception--distortion tension between video methods and per-frame restorers on the reference-based axes. On \textbf{L1} SDSD-outdoor, Seer ties Retinexformer for the PSNR lead ($22.03$ vs.\ $22.00$~dB, within the seed-variance floor) and wins LPIPS outright ($29\%$ over Retinexformer); on \textbf{L2} BVI-RLV, Seer catches DarkIR on PSNR ($20.39$~dB, within $0.07$~dB), holds the temporal-quality frontier (best tLP, essentially at GT), and lands second on CLIPIQA. Zero-shot L3--L5 LOL results---the L3 PSNR/DISTS crown ($23.45$~dB, $.078$) and second-best CLIPIQA on L4/L5---validate that the V-JEPA video prior transfers to still images without any LOL-specific tuning.

\noindent\textbf{An illumination-invariant video world model prior.} We show V-JEPA is the most illumination-invariant of five widely-used encoders on single frames and, via a predictive-coherence test its four per-frame competitors cannot even be subjected to, the only one with a verified capacity to forecast future spatio-temporal dynamics; we identify it as a natural, overlooked prior for cross-domain LLVE.

\noindent\textbf{Seer.} The first LLVE framework built around a frozen video world model, coupling a Restormer-style pixel branch to V-JEPA through three complementary mechanisms.

\noindent\textbf{Training-free Bayesian chroma preservation.} A per-channel LMMSE fusion admitting biased, correlated and non-Gaussian residuals; the operational coefficient is the closed-form plug-in Bayesian estimator on empirical residual second moments, matching the empirical PSNR optimum within $0.005$~dB.
